JPN Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how information may be collected, used, stored and disclosed when you visit the JPN website, create or use a JPN account, submit a plan request or Enterprise inquiry, use JPN downloads or setup resources, contact support, or use JPN-related network services. JPN is an independently operated, app-focused private-networking service for individuals and organisations. It is not affiliated with any school, college or other institution.

IP address and connection notice: Website, authentication, API, hosting, security and network providers may process IP addresses and technical connection information. Depending on system configuration and permissions, authorised JPN operators or administrators may be able to access some IP addresses, timestamps, account identifiers and diagnostic logs. JPN does not promise anonymous browsing or that connection information is never logged.

1. Information JPN may process

  • Account and sign-in information: email address, account identifier, first and last name supplied during registration, authentication status, account recovery events and security-related account information. Authentication providers process passwords as part of sign-in; JPN does not need to receive or display your plain-text password.
  • Profile information: display name and profile details you choose to save.
  • Plan and membership information: requested plan, request status and timestamp, membership or entitlement status, plan type, payment confirmation or administrative access records where maintained, and information needed to control access to downloads. A plan request may be stored in account metadata.
  • Enterprise inquiry information: the name, work email address, organisation and message you submit through the Enterprise inquiry form, together with information needed to respond to you.
  • Support communications: information you include when emailing JPN support, including your email address, device or operating system details, the issue you describe and any files you choose to attach. Please do not email passwords or private JPN configuration files.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, timestamps, browser and device information, request metadata, error reports, security events, diagnostic logs and service activity, where collected by JPN or its providers.
  • Cookie and browser storage information: your cookie preference, essential browser storage used for sign-in and website operation, and whether you have dismissed a site announcement during the current browser tab session, as described below.
  • Network connection metadata: information processed by the network infrastructure and third-party client or service used to establish a JPN connection. The exact information depends on the client, network configuration and provider logging practices.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and authenticate accounts; recover accounts and secure sign-in; maintain profiles; review plan requests and manage memberships; verify eligibility for downloads; provide configuration and setup resources; respond to Enterprise inquiries and support requests; maintain and troubleshoot the website, API and network service; prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access; investigate security incidents; communicate service changes; and comply with legal obligations.

JPN does not currently advertise optional analytics or advertising cookies on the website. Information is not collected for those purposes merely because you select “Accept” in the cookie notice.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The JPN website displays a cookie preference notice with “Accept” and “Essential only” choices. The choice is saved in browser local storage under jpn_cookie_consent_v1 and, where available, in a first-party cookie named jpn_cookie_consent. The cookie can last for up to one year and is scoped to the JPN website path. The choice records your preference; it does not currently enable optional analytics or advertising cookies because those are not enabled on the site.

JPN and its authentication provider may also use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sign-in sessions, support account security and make the website work. The announcement banner feature uses session storage in your browser tab to remember which announcements you dismissed; that dismissal record is temporary and is not used for analytics or advertising. Blocking or clearing these technologies may sign you out, reset your cookie choice or cause dismissed announcements to appear again. You can change your preference using the “Cookie settings” control where shown, or clear JPN site data in your browser settings.

4. Providers and disclosures

JPN uses or may use independent providers to operate the website and services. Current project infrastructure includes GitHub Pages for website hosting, Supabase for authentication and account-related data, and Render for backend/API services. JPN may also rely on third-party client software such as v2rayN, network infrastructure, email services and other providers as features change.

Those providers may process information on JPN’s behalf or for their own operational, security and legal purposes under their own terms and privacy notices. They may store or process information in Australia or overseas, depending on their infrastructure and settings. JPN does not control all provider systems, locations, security practices or retention periods. Review the relevant provider notices for further information.

Information may be disclosed to people who administer or support JPN where access is reasonably needed to operate the service, handle a request, troubleshoot a problem or protect security. Information may also be disclosed where required or authorised by law, or where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected misuse, prevent harm or protect JPN, users or others.

5. IP addresses and network activity

When you visit the website or use an account feature, hosting, authentication, API, security and other providers may process your IP address, timestamps, request details and related technical information. When you use JPN network services, relevant network operators and third-party client providers may process connection metadata. The information available to JPN administrators depends on the deployed configuration and the access permissions they have.

JPN does not promise that it can see every connection detail, that it cannot see any connection detail, or that all network activity is unlogged. JPN does not guarantee anonymity, concealment from your internet provider or destination services, or protection against all tracking. This notice does not claim that JPN routinely reads the contents of users’ communications.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this notice, including account operation, membership administration, support, security, dispute handling and legal compliance. Exact retention periods vary across JPN systems and third-party providers, and JPN does not currently publish a single fixed retention period for every category of information. Some information may remain in provider backups or security logs for a period determined by that provider.

JPN uses account controls and provider security features intended to protect information, but no website, network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password and private configuration files confidential and for using secure devices.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage certain profile and security details through your JPN dashboard, including your display name, sign-in email and password. You can choose whether to accept the cookie notice or use essential storage only. You may also stop using JPN and contact the operator about your account.

For a request to access, correct or delete personal information, or to ask a privacy question, email jigar@jigar.au from the address associated with your account where possible. Explain what information or account the request concerns. JPN may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution or service-integrity reasons, or may remain temporarily in provider backups. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when JPN features, providers or legal obligations change. The date and version at the top identify the current published notice. Material changes will be reflected on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact jigar@jigar.au.
