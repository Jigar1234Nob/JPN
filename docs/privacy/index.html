Privacy Notice

This notice describes information that may be processed when you visit JPN, an independently operated app-focused technology project designed for the St Peter’s College Cranbourne West Campus community, create an account, or use its account portal and related services. JPN is not an official College service unless formally authorised by the College.

IP address notice: JPN hosting, authentication, API, security and other providers may process IP addresses and technical connection logs. Authorized people with access to those systems or logs may be able to view some of that information.

Cookies and browser storage

JPN displays a cookie preference notice. Your choice is saved in browser storage and in a first-party cookie named jpn_cookie_consent where cookies are available. It records whether you selected “Accept” or “Essential only” and lasts for up to one year. JPN also uses essential browser storage and authentication-related storage required for account sign-in and service operation. Optional analytics and advertising cookies are not currently enabled by this site. You can reopen the notice using the “Cookie settings” control shown after making a choice, or clear site data in your browser settings.

Information that may be processed

  • Account details such as your email address, account identifier and authentication status.
  • Technical data such as IP address, timestamps, browser/device details, request metadata and diagnostic or security logs, where collected by JPN or its providers.
  • Information you submit to support or account forms, including the name, email address, organization and message entered in an Enterprise inquiry.
  • Service and security events used to troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse and protect accounts.

Why information may be used

Information may be used to operate and secure accounts, respond to Enterprise inquiries, discuss service requirements, send account-related messages, diagnose technical problems, prevent misuse, comply with applicable law and maintain service reliability.

Providers and network services

JPN may use independent providers such as GitHub Pages, Supabase and Render to deliver the account portal, application downloads and related services. These providers may process information under their own policies and technical configurations. If a JPN application connects to network infrastructure or third-party services, connection metadata may be visible to the relevant operators. No application guarantees anonymity or prevents all tracking.

Retention, security and sharing

Retention periods vary by system and provider. Access to information should be limited to people and services that need it for operation, support or security, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. Information may be disclosed when legally required or when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or secure the service.

Your choices and requests

Use the account controls where available to manage your account. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact the JPN operator through a contact method published on the website, if available. Applicable privacy and consumer laws may give you additional rights.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as JPN's services and providers change. The updated date above indicates the latest version published here.

This notice is a project draft, not legal advice. Review it against the actual deployment, provider settings and applicable Australian privacy obligations before treating it as a final privacy policy.
